# MidnightBotAI - change notes (plain language)

All notable changes to the bot system, newest first. Written for players, not developers.

## 4.8
- Tanks now open fights: the paladin judges from a distance, the warrior charges in, and
  tanks learn and use their taunt so mobs are pulled off the player and the rest of the team.
- Damage dealers hold their fire until the tank has built threat; they still defend the
  player (or themselves) instantly if attacked.

## 4.7
- Personal space: every bot has its own standing spot, casters retreat to opposite sides when
  attacked in melee, and any bot that ends up on top of another steps aside.

## 4.6
- Bots spend their talent points automatically as soon as talents become available.

## 4.5
- Level sync: bots level up together with the player.
- Auto gear: bots equip level-appropriate upgrades for their class.
- Line of sight: if a target breaks line of sight, bots reposition to re-acquire it.
- Focus fire: damage dealers follow the tank's target; casters keep range; healers prioritize
  the tank.

## 4.3
- Fifth team member support plus improved paladin and warlock ability routines.
- Fixed the boost-all command so it reliably levels the whole team.

## 4.2
- Damage and healing meters (in-game stats command) and group loot rules
  (view/set method and quality threshold).

## 4.0
- Combat feel: bot abilities now have cast animations and sounds, and hit effects; damage
  numbers are capped to sensible values instead of instant-kill spikes.

## 3.x
- Reliable bot damage and healing; auto-binding to the owner; follow and assist enabled
  automatically; cast wind-up timing; per-bot damage diagnostics.

## 2.x
- Bot roster persistence, party formation, target acquisition, role behavior
  (tank, healer, damage) and healing.

## 1.x
- First working spawn: characters created, logged in without a game client, following the
  owner and fighting together.
